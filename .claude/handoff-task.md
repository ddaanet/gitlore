## Current task

The redesign of gitlore tier propagation is settled and specced at `docs/plans/2026-07-30-13-tier-pinning-composition-projection.md`; no code is written yet. Tiers stay pinned at the gitlink recorded in the memory submodule and are never fast-forwarded, so root's index and a tier's carrier cannot diverge outside an explicit merge. The three-way stays where it belongs — the merge, in `scripts/lib/index-merge.sh`, keyed on the pointer path with per-entry diff3 — and composition drops its degraded copy of that resolver in favour of two projections: root's active tier lines down into each carrier during a session, and the adopted carrier up into root's tier block once, at a tier-adoption step inside the merge command that does not exist today. `refs/gitlore/compose-base` goes with the merge it served. Four dependency-ordered steps with file:line anchors are in the plan.
